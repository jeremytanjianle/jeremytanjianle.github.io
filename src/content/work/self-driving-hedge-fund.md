## Research that keeps moving

The Self-driving Hedge Fund is a new investigation into autonomous financial research: systems that can gather evidence, test hypotheses, propose actions and learn from their outcomes while keeping decisions inspectable.

The project is currently in development. Architecture notes, experiments and results will be published here as they become ready.

## Design principles

- Evidence before action
- Explicit risk constraints
- Reproducible research trails
- Human oversight for consequential decisions
